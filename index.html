Refund, Resend & Returns Policy

We stand behind every order we ship. Below is a clear breakdown of when refunds, replacements, and resends apply — and what we may need from you to process them.

1. Delayed or Lost Orders

We understand how frustrating it is to wait for a package. If your order is delayed or missing tracking updates, we're here to help. The following timeframes apply before we can open a formal claim with our logistics partner:

  • Most countries: claims may be opened 60 days after the order departs our warehouse.
  • United States: claims may be opened 45 days after dispatch.
  • Brazil: due to strict customs clearance, claims may be opened 110 days after dispatch.
  • Liquid or restricted items: claims may be opened 100 days after dispatch.

Please note:

  • Packages sometimes arrive at your local post office but show as "pending" due to an incomplete address, a missed pickup, or a failed delivery attempt. In these cases, contacting your local post office directly is often the fastest way to receive your item.
  • In some regions (including Israel), packages are frequently routed to self-service pickup lockers, which can lead to long delays if not collected promptly.
  • Occasionally, tracking shows "Delivered" but the customer reports not receiving the package. We'll investigate every case thoroughly, but resolution can take 1–2 months, and a successful outcome cannot be guaranteed.

2. Packages Marked "Delivered" or with Tracking Alerts

We're unable to process a refund or resend if tracking shows the package as delivered, unless you can provide a non-delivery certificate issued by your local post office with an official seal.

If tracking shows an Alert, this typically means one of the following:

  • Incorrect or incomplete address
  • No such street number
  • Unknown recipient
  • Recipient refused delivery
  • Package not picked up in time
  • No safe delivery location
  • Package held in customs

Please note:

  • Carriers typically attempt delivery 1–3 times before returning the package to the local post office. If the package remains unclaimed, it will be held for 3–7 days before being returned to us. We cannot guarantee against loss during return transit.
  • If a package is returned to us because it was undeliverable, we're unable to offer a refund or resend without prior arrangement.
  • Packages destroyed by the carrier are not eligible for a refund.

3. Damaged Products

If your order arrives damaged, we've got you covered:

  • Fully damaged packages: full refund or replacement.
  • Partially damaged packages: partial refund or replacement (excluding minor cosmetic issues such as loose threads, slight wrinkles, or small scratches).

Please note:

  • Cosmetic damage to the outer shipping box does not qualify for a refund, as this is common with international delivery.
  • Please open a claim within 30 days of delivery for damaged or defective items.

4. Wrong or Missing Products

Every order passes strict quality control before dispatch, but if something isn't right when it arrives, we'll make it right:

  • Wrong product received: full refund or replacement.
  • Wrong color or size (where the item still functions as intended): refund or replacement, provided a photo or note is included.
  • Missing part that doesn't affect function: partial refund or replacement of the missing part.
  • Missing part that affects function: full replacement of the product.
  • Missing accessory: we'll resend the accessory at no cost to you.

For size-related concerns, please measure the product using the standard measurement method and include a clear photo of the measurement — this helps us resolve your case much faster.

5. Order Cancellation

  • Before your order is processed by our warehouse: full refund available on request.
  • After your order is processed: cancellation is no longer possible, as the item is already being prepared for shipment.
To open a claim: contact our support team with your order number, a description of the issue, and any supporting photos or documents.
